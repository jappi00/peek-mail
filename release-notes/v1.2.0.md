## What's new in Peek Mail 1.2.0

- **Quick Look in Finder.** Select an Outlook `.msg` file and press Space to see the whole message without opening any app. Previews run sandboxed without network access, so remote images never load.
- **`winmail.dat` unpacked.** Mails from Outlook or Exchange that hide their body and attachments in a TNEF `winmail.dat` part now show their real content and attachments.
- **Find in the message.** Press ⌘F to search the rendered mail, with a match counter and ↑/↓ to jump between results.
- **Print and export as PDF.** ⌘P prints the message and ⇧⌘E saves it as a PDF, with sender, recipients, date and the attachment list above the body.
- **No more freezes on large files.** Mails are read and parsed in the background.
- **Roomier history search** in the sidebar.

### Good to know

For `.eml` files, macOS uses Apple Mail's Quick Look preview by default. To use Peek Mail's instead, turn off Mail under System Settings → General → Login Items & Extensions → Quick Look.
